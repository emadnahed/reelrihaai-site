ReelRihaai — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

ReelRihaai helps you spend less time on endless feeds (Reels, Shorts, Status) by pausing them and showing you your own week. It is built so that nothing about you leaves your phone.

What ReelRihaai uses, and why

WhatPlatformWhyWhere it goes
Accessibility serviceAndroidTo notice when you are on a Reels, Shorts or Status screen inside the apps you chose, so the feed can be paused. It reads only the screen's layout (which screen is open), never your messages, posts, contacts or anything you type.Stays on your phone
Usage accessAndroidTo show how much time goes into those apps each day, and how your week compares with your first week.Stays on your phone
Screen Time (Family Controls)iOSTo pause the apps you choose. Apple does not share which apps or websites you use with ReelRihaai.Stays on your phone
NotificationsBothSetup reminders, a note when the first days are over, an optional Sunday reflection, and an alert if pausing stops working.Scheduled on your phone
Your choicesBothWhich apps and feeds to pause, your commitment level, your weekly pages.Stored on your phone

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, send or sell any personal data. ReelRihaai has no servers and no accounts.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not read the content of your messages, posts, photos or anything you type.

Your control

  • Turn off the accessibility service, usage access or Screen Time at any time in your phone's settings. ReelRihaai keeps working with whatever you allow.
  • Uninstalling ReelRihaai deletes everything it stored.

Children

ReelRihaai is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
