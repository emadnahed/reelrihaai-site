Common questions

Pausing stopped working on Android. Open Settings → Accessibility → ReelRihaai and make sure it is on. Some phones turn it off after updates or battery optimisation; set ReelRihaai's battery use to "Unrestricted".

The shield doesn't appear on iPhone. Check Settings → Screen Time is on and ReelRihaai is allowed under Screen Time access, then re-choose your apps in ReelRihaai.

How do I lower my commitment level? Change it in Settings inside the app. Lowering takes effect after 24 hours, by design.

Where is my data? Only on your phone. Uninstalling deletes it. See the privacy policy.
